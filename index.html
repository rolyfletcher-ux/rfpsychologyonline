Counselling & Psychotherapy

with Dr Roly Fletcher

Sometimes life, relationships, work or our social life can leave us feeling confused, unhappy, trapped, overwhelmed or struggling to cope.

Therapy provides a confidential setting where these things can be discussed to take stock and consider how to move forward.

As it's not always convenient to travel to a consulting room, sessions are online, all that's needed is a computer with a webcam. Sessions are normally 50 minutes long, held regularly at an agreed time allowing a space in which whatever is troubling you can be talked about and worked on.

Over time therapy can bring greater understanding of these issues, how to deal with them, and how you feel about them.
