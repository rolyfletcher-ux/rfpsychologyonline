Counselling & Psychotherapy

with Dr Roly Fletcher

Experience

I have been working as a therapist in private practice for over 10 years. I have worked clinically in the NHS, on the teaching faculty of the doctorate training programme in Counselling and Psychotherapeutic Psychology at the University of Surrey, and provided clinical and teaching support for a number of charities, groups, and individuals.
   In addition to offering therapeutic work, I provide consultation services/supervision to professionals.

Training

I undertook my initial clinical training at the University of Surrey (doctorate in Psychotherapeutic and Counselling Psychology).

Since then I have undertaken a number of additional courses to further develop my insight and understanding of how to work with the mind such as, psychodynamic psychotherapy (with the WPF in London), supervision training with both the doctorate in Clinical Psychology and doctorate in Psychotherapeutic and Counselling Psychology (both at the University of Surrey).
